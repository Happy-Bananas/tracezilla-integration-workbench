# Create a Shopify Partner account

A Shopify Partner account gives an integration consultant access to Shopify's
developer tools, including Partner organizations, development stores, and
applications. You do not need a customer's production store to complete this
guide.

If your company already has a Partner organization, ask its owner to invite you
instead of creating another organization.

## Create or join an organization

1. Open the [Shopify Partner Dashboard](https://partners.shopify.com/).
2. Sign in with your Shopify account or create one.
3. Join your company's existing Partner organization if it is available.
4. Otherwise, choose the option to create a Partner organization.
5. Select the business activity that includes building stores or applications
   for clients.
6. Enter the requested company, contact, and regional information.
7. Complete any email or identity verification requested by Shopify.

Shopify changes its onboarding questions periodically. The important outcome
is access to a Partner organization where you can see **Stores** and **Apps**.

## Verify the result

Return to the Partner Dashboard and confirm that:

- The intended Partner organization is selected.
- You can open the store-management area.
- You can open the app-development area.
- Your role permits you to create a development store and an app.

If those areas are unavailable, ask the organization owner to review your
Partner permissions before continuing.
